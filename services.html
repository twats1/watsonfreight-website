Our Capabilities

Freight solutions built around your load.

Flatbed Transportation

Dependable open-deck transportation for freight that needs side, top, or crane loading. We secure and transport materials with care from pickup through delivery.

  • Lumber and building materials
  • Steel, pipe, and fabricated products
  • Machinery and industrial freight
  • Jobsite and construction deliveries
02

Step-Deck Transportation

A practical solution for taller freight requiring additional clearance while maintaining the loading flexibility of an open-deck trailer.

  • Tall machinery and equipment
  • Fabricated structures
  • Crated industrial freight
  • Specialized cargo
03

Regional Freight

Responsive regional transportation serving Mississippi and the Southeast, with direct communication and dependable scheduling.

  • Single-load shipments
  • Recurring freight lanes
  • Customer and jobsite deliveries
  • Time-sensitive regional freight

Open-Deck Experience

The right equipment for demanding freight.

Open-deck freight requires careful planning, proper securement, and an understanding of how different materials should be handled. Watson Freight brings practical experience to every shipment.

We work with shippers, receivers, and jobsites to keep pickup and delivery expectations clear. When conditions change, we communicate quickly so everyone knows what to expect.
